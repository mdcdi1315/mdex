

### Teleporter Spawn Data Command Group

Commands under this group modify or query - on one way 
or another - the Teleporting Manager spawn data applied.

Those commands can be accessed by accessing the sub-command `teleporter_spawn_data`:

~~~
/mdex teleporter_spawn_data
~~~
